# Kinpaw v1

> **Status: done, 2026-10-02.** Merged to `main` (PR #7). Runs locally: the iOS app on the
> Simulator and an iPhone, the web server and a local Supabase stack.

## What Kinpaw is

A cozy AI pet that lives on your phone. You design it, care for its four needs every day
(hunger, happiness, energy, affection) and talk to it by voice. It answers in character. The
core feeling is "someone missed me".

**Stack:** an Expo / React Native iOS app, a Next.js server and admin panel, Postgres (Supabase
locally) through Prisma, and Groq's `openai/gpt-oss-20b` for the pet's replies.

## Features

### Create your pet

- **What it does:** from a fresh install, before signing in, you pick a ready-made friend,
  customize it, or build your own (species, fur, pattern, eyes, ears, body, tail). Then you
  name it and give it a personality: a preset, or six traits tuned by hand.
- **How it works:** every pet is drawn in code from its attributes (`PetArt`), so any
  combination works with every mood and no images are needed. The draft is kept on the phone
  until you sign in.

### Save your pet and sign in

- **What it does:** you sign in with a code sent by email, and your pet is saved to your
  account. "I already have an account" brings back the pet you saved before.
- **How it works:** Supabase Auth checks who you are, and the server saves the pet to the
  `pets` table. Saving is safe to retry. If the account already has a pet, that pet is kept
  and you're told so gently.

### Home: caring for your pet

- **What it does:** your pet sits under a sky that follows the real time of day. The four needs
  are pills beside it, and Feed, Play and Pet are below. The pet reacts with hearts, treats and
  stars, and says what it wants in its speech bubble. Tapping a need opens a sheet that explains
  it in plain words.
- **How it works:** needs live in the `pet_needs` table. The server works out how far they have
  dropped since your last visit, then applies the action, in one locked transaction. The app
  updates the screen straight away and rolls back if the server fails. The needs rules are
  written once in `packages/contract` and shared by the app and the server.

### The needs rhythm

- **What it does:** needs drop through the day, much more slowly at night (23:00–07:00, when
  the pet sleeps and gets its energy back). Care gives less as a need fills up.
- **How it works:** daytime hourly drop: hunger −12, happiness −9, affection −7, energy −5. At
  night needs drop at a quarter of that, and energy goes up +12 an hour. The server keeps
  fractions and the app shows whole numbers.

### Talk to your pet

- **What it does:** hold the mic on Home and speak, or type. The pet answers in one or two
  sentences in its bubble, in its own personality, and talking raises its needs. A harsh message
  hurts it, but it forgives. It uses your name. Free accounts get 10 messages a day.
- **How it works:**
  - Speech is turned into text on the phone (`expo-speech-recognition`), with a 20-word limit.
  - The server builds a prompt from the pet's traits, needs and mood and the last three
    exchanges, then asks Groq. The answer is checked with Zod (a reply line and an intent).
  - The intent maps to need changes through a fixed table, so the model never sets numbers
    itself.
  - The daily limit is counted on the server and resets at local midnight. A retried message
    is never counted twice.
  - Nothing you say is stored, and `ai_usage` records the cost.

### My pet, Profile and Settings

- **What it does:** a details page shows the pet's look and coloured trait bars, and lets you
  change its look. Profile has your name and photo. Settings covers your account, including
  deleting it.
- **How it works:** photos are uploaded to a private storage bucket through signed upload
  URLs, and the name is saved through `PATCH /api/app/v1/profile`.

### Kinpaw Plus (premium)

- **What it does:** Plus gives unlimited talking and extra looks for the pet. There's a
  paywall, locked looks open it, and Profile shows a "Plus member" panel.
- **How it works:**
  - Only the server decides who has Plus (`plan_type` on `profiles`). The app re-reads it when
    you return to the app, when you open Home and when the paywall closes.
  - Admins can grant or revoke Plus on `/admin/users`, so it can be demoed without
    RevenueCat. Each grant and revoke is recorded in `premium_grants`.

### Polish

- **What it does:** VoiceOver labels, 44pt tap targets, support for Reduce Motion and large
  text, and the marketing website brought in line with the app.
