## Long-term memory

- **What it does:** the pet remembers short facts you tell it ("exam on Friday", "sister called
  Ece") and brings them up later ("How did the exam go?"). You can see, search and forget
  everything it remembers. "Tell me about you", right after you save your pet, lets you say a few
  sentences so it knows you from the first minute.
- **How it works:**
  - When the pet replies, the same AI call may keep one new fact. It never keeps the conversation
    and never anything sensitive (health, religion, passwords and the like are filtered out).
  - Up to 30 facts per pet, 100 with Plus. For each message, a score in plain code (matching
    words, a follow-up that's due, importance) picks the 6 facts the AI sees, so the prompt stays
    small: about a cent a month per user.
  - "Tell me about you": up to 60 words, spoken or typed. One AI call turns them into up to 8
    facts and a first line from the pet. Nothing is sent for someone who says they're under 13.
- **How it's built:** the `pet_memories` and intro-attempts tables; the memory rules and scoring
  on the server; talking on Groq's `gpt-oss-120b` (moved from `gpt-oss-20b`, which couldn't
  update a fact that changed). In the app: "Mochi will remember that" under a reply, "From
  Mochi's memory" when a reply uses one (the server checks it really does), "Mochi remembers…"
  on the pet's page with Manage all, and the "Tell me about you" page.
- **How well it works:** against Groq, 12 of 12 facts worth keeping were kept and 0 of 8
  sensitive ones. A real try kept 6 facts from one paragraph and left out depression, therapy,
  religion and a password.
- **"I missed you"** (2026-10-05): come back after 6 hours or more and the pet's first line
  says so, by how long you were away ("You're back! I missed you. Not even pretending."); an
  event it remembers whose day has come is asked about once ("I missed you, Mert! How did the
  exam go?"). No AI call: the server says how long you were away and which question is due.
- **Next:** Mert's check of "I missed you", then the finishing pass.

## Notes from your pet

- **What it does:** when you're away and the pet starts to miss you, a note in its voice appears
  on your lock screen ("I saved you the warm spot"), titled with its name. Tapping it opens Home.
- **How it works:**
  - The phone works out when a need will drop low and schedules the note itself. No server, no
    push token, no AI call: the lines come from a written bank per need and species.
  - Never sooner than 3 hours after you leave, only between 09:00 and 21:00, at most one a day
    and three in all. Opening the app cancels them and starts over.
  - The pet asks first, after your first Feed, Play or Pet, never at launch. "Notes from Mochi"
    in Settings turns them on or off.
  - A note never mentions anything the pet remembers.
- **How it's built:** `expo-notifications` (a native module, so it needs a rebuild), the schedule
  and the lines on the phone, the toggle in Settings, and a line in the privacy pages.
- **Counting the opens** (2026-10-06): a tapped note is counted by the same greeting call that
  says "I missed you" (`notification_opened`, with its day and need), and so is whether notes
  can reach you (`notifications_state`), so open rates come from our own numbers.
- **Next:** Mert's check of the opens on the iPhone.
- **Spec:** [requirements](../features/v2-notifications-requirements.md) ·
  [plan](../features/v2-notifications-plan.md)

## More pet looks and a layered coat

- **What it does:** more choices in Make your own, and coats with more than one colour, so you
  can make a pet that looks like your own: a tabby with white socks, a calico, a Siamese, a husky,
  a beagle, a Doberman.
- **How it works:** a coat is now four choices: colour, pattern, marking colour and paws & chest
  (white markings in any fur colour). New: 3 furs, 3 eye colours, 2 ears per species, 2 tails, a
  Tiny body and 5 patterns. Lynx ears and Ice eyes are Plus.
- **How it's built:** both apps draw the pet from one shared list of shapes (`petDrawing` in
  `@app/brand`), so the app and the website always match. Three new `pets` columns (`accent`,
  `white`, `trim`); saved pets look exactly as before. Make your own also got about 4× lighter
  taps and lost a 1–2 second glitch on each pick.
- **Spec:** [requirements](../features/v2-pet-looks-requirements.md) ·
  [plan](../features/v2-pet-looks-plan.md)

## Your pet from a photo

- **What it does:** on Start, **From a photo** turns a photo of your real cat or dog into your
  Kinpaw pet. You watch it draw itself, check it ("Is this your pet?"), then keep it or adjust
  it. With Kinpaw Plus you can redo your pet from a new photo once a day in Change look, even
  from a dog to a cat. Start now offers From a photo or Make your own (Choose a friend is gone).
- **How it works:**
  - The phone shrinks the photo to a few hundred KB before sending it. Our server strips its
    location, then one call to Google's Gemini 2.5 Flash (through fal.ai) describes the pet using
    our own list of options. Our code draws it. About 2 seconds and $0.001 a photo. The photo is
    never kept.
  - The first photo asks your permission to send it to the AI, naming the service.
  - Fair use: 2 tries per phone while signed out (deleting the app doesn't reset them), 6 a day
    per network, 300 a day for the whole app. Only real reads count.
- **How it's built:** the read and redo routes, a `pet_photo_reads` table that counts tries by
  scrambled codes (never the phone's id or the address), and the photo screens in the app. The
  Plus check is on the server.
- **How well it works:** on Mert's 6 photos, 36 of 36 right on species, coat, pattern, markings,
  eyes and ears. Every screen was checked on the Simulator against the real model.
- **Next:** Mert's check on the iPhone; the camera and photo permission text says "your pet" at
  the next native rebuild.
- **Spec:** [requirements](../features/v2-pet-from-photo-requirements.md) ·
  [plan](../features/v2-pet-from-photo-plan.md)

## Mochi speaks

- **What it does:** when the pet answers you, a moment after the text it says the reply out loud,
  in a voice that fits it: eight voices, by cat or dog and personality.
- **How it works:**
  - Inworld TTS-1.5 Max on fal.ai: about $0.001 a reply (under $1 a month even for a heavy Plus
    user), about 2 seconds to make. The phone plays it 1.15× faster without pitch correction so
    the pet sounds small. MiniMax Speech 2.8 Turbo, with pitch and emotion, was the first pick
    and stays one setting away (`VOICE_PROVIDER=minimax`), at six times the price.
  - Only a reply the server just counted can be spoken, once; at most 5 a day free, 20 with Plus, and 1,000 for
    the whole app a day. A voice that fails or hits a limit leaves the text as it was.
  - Through the speaker, even after the mic (iOS otherwise uses the quiet earpiece). Speak to the
    pet and it answers aloud even with the silent switch on, like Siri; type, and it follows the
    switch. Not played with VoiceOver on.
  - "Mochi's voice" in Settings, on by default; off sends nothing.
- **Before Home** (2026-10-06): the personality page says the personality shapes the voice, and
  tapping one plays it; Meet says its first line out loud as the pet lands; "Tell me about you"
  speaks the pet's answer. The fixed lines are recorded once and shipped in the app (no cost per
  play, works before sign-in).
- **How it's built:** `POST /pet/voice` on the server, the voice picked from the pet; `expo-audio`
  on the phone, already in the build, so no rebuild. `npm run voice:check` plays all eight voices
  to tune them by ear.
- **Next:** Mert hears the eight voices and tries it on the iPhone; then whether it stays free
  or becomes a Plus feature.
- **Spec:** [requirements](../features/v2-voice-requirements.md) ·
  [plan](../features/v2-voice-plan.md)

## Friends

- **What it does:** a Friends tab, second. Show your QR code; a friend scans it with their
  iPhone's Camera (or types the code, or taps a shared link), sees whose pet it is, and adds you.
  Then each of you sees the other's pet in its mood, and how it is doing in words.
- **How it works:**
  - The QR holds a `cozypet://` link, so the iPhone's own Camera opens Kinpaw on the add screen:
    no camera code in the app and no native rebuild.
  - A code is 8 characters with no look-alikes (no 0/O, 1/I/L), made by the server; New code
    stops the old one. Friendship is mutual at once, up to 50 each; removing ends it for both.
  - A friend sees your display name and your pet only: its look, name, mood and needs as low /
    okay / good. Never your email, photo, memories, what you said, or Plus.
- **How it's built:** the `friend_codes` and `friendships` tables; five routes under
  `/api/app/v1/friends`; a strict response shape so nothing else can leak. In the app: the tab,
  the My code sheet, the add screen, a friend's pet page, and links that arrive before the pet
  is saved kept for a day.
- **Next:** Mert's check on the Simulator and the iPhone, with two accounts.
- **Spec:** [requirements](../features/v2-friends-requirements.md) ·
  [plan](../features/v2-friends-plan.md)

## The website, rebuilt

- **What it does:** the landing page is now a week with a pet, "Seven days with Mochi", in place
  of the v1 page (a phone beside a headline, a five-act story, three feature panes). Each day is a
  full-width sky at its own hour and a thing you can do:
  - **Day 1** (the sky at your own hour): Mochi, alive. Feed, Play and Pet work, by the app's own
    rules.
  - **Day 2** (morning): the eight voices, the app's own recordings.
  - **Day 3** (evening): a scripted replay of a talk in which the pet keeps a fact, voiced.
  - **Days 4 and 6** pass in one line each: nothing happens, nobody keeps score.
  - **Day 5** (afternoon): the lock-screen note, and what a note is allowed to be.
  - **Day 7** (evening): "I missed you! How did the exam go?", from its memory, voiced.
  - Then **Make yours** (the app's pet builder, on the page), **the app itself** (six real
    screenshots and a 30-second recording), the plans, the questions, and a closing night scene
    where the pet you made is asleep and the button says "Save <name> a spot".
- **A page for reviewers:** `/whats-new` says what v2 added, how each part works, the measured
  results, the fair-use limits and what is sent where. Pricing and the FAQ are rewritten for v2
  (spoken replies, memory sizes, the Plus photo redo; four new questions).
- **How it works:**
  - The pet on day 1 and the builder use the shared code (`applyAction` and the catalogue in
    `@app/contract`, the drawing in `@app/brand`), so the site cannot drift from the app.
  - The skies are the app's colours (`art.sky`), turned into CSS by `src/lib/sky.ts`. A scene
    takes the scheme its hour takes in the app, so the page goes light and dark as the week passes.
  - Sound only ever starts from a tap. The 24 clips in `public/voice` are copies of the app's;
    the two lines of the replay are recorded by `npm run voice:site --workspace=@app/web`.
  - The screenshots and the recording (`public/app`, listed in `src/lib/captures.ts`) are from the
    iPhone 17 Pro Simulator with a test account, nothing staged. The Simulator records no sound,
    so the recording's two spoken replies were made afterwards from the text on screen, with the
    app's own voice provider, and laid in where the sound bars move.
- **How it's measured:** `week_day_viewed` (day 7 over `landing_view` is the page's number, decided
  with Mert), `hero_pet_cared`, `voice_sample_played`, `memory_replay_played`, `pet_builder_used`,
  `demo_video_played`, `whats_new_view`. `story_view` went with the old story.
- **How it was designed:** with the impeccable skill (a Claude Code plugin, not in the repo). Its
  record of the direction is `apps/web/.impeccable/surfaces/`; the old contract embedded in the
  page's HTML is gone.
- **Not done:** "from a photo" is described, not demonstrated (no pet photo to publish yet); the
  paywall screenshot is left out, because a development build shows preview prices; the privacy
  page has no paragraph on memory, voice or notes yet.

## Code quality

- **Why:** the week-1 scorecard gave code quality 3/5. An audit of the whole repo on 2026-10-07
  found a strong base (one route factory, one shared contract, transactions with locks, strict
  types, every gate green) and the points lost in what a reviewer sees first and a few very large
  screens.
- **What changed (2026-10-08):**
  - **First impressions:** the three READMEs rewritten for Kinpaw (screenshots, an architecture
    diagram, the quality gates, how to run it); stray template files removed.
  - **Correctness:** the sign-in code now goes to the address as typed (it used to strip `+tags`
    and Gmail dots); sign-in errors are plain words, never Supabase's own text; a dropped connection
    no longer counts as a wrong code toward the lockout.
  - **Smaller screens:** Home 734 → 508 lines, login 880 → 268, Create · Start 725 → 147. Their
    logic moved into small hooks and pure functions (what the bubble says, the pet's reactions,
    the notes ask, the emailed-code flow, after sign-in…), each with tests.
  - **Tests:** 1,037 → 1,145. The optimistic care (Feed, Play, Pet), talking, and the admin's
    Plus grant (now proven to refuse anyone who isn't an admin, before anything is read or
    written) are covered; the key tests were checked to fail when the code they guard is removed.
- **How it was done:** pure moves, no change to what a screen shows; every step ended with all of
  CI's gates green and a check on the Simulator or the iPhone.
- **Next:** Phase 4, consistency (environment reads through one validated module, input checks on
  the template routes, dead code), then the launch notes.

  [plan](../features/v2-code-quality-plan.md)
