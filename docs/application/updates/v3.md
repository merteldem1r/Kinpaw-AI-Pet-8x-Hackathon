## Pet looks: a living pet

- **What it does:** the pet stops being a still sticker. Its tail moves the way its species
  does, its ears twitch and perk up, it tilts its head to listen while you hold the mic, and its
  mouth moves when it talks. Touching its head, chin, nose, belly, paw or tail each gets its own
  reaction, and holding it is a hug. Then the same pet is drawn in 2.5D: lit by the sky's light,
  round, with soft fur and a head that turns.
- **How it works:** one set of animated values per pet (its pose), set by one piece of behaviour
  code and read by the drawing. First today's SVG drawing reads it; then a Skia drawing (the GPU)
  reads the same pose and adds light and fur with shaders.
- **Spec:** [requirements and tracking](../features/v3-pet-looks-requirements.md),
  [plan](../features/v3-pet-looks-plan.md), [research](../features/v3-pet-looks-research.md).
- **Log:**
  - 2026-10-09: researched (the market, Rive, 3D, Skia) and specced; direction agreed with Mert:
    alive and soft, built in code, Part A (SVG) then Part B (Skia).
  - 2026-10-09: the events for touch registered (`zone` on `pet_tapped` and `pet_stroked`,
    `pet_hugged`). The pet is now drawn in parts (tail, body, head, each ear) that can each
    move, with nothing visibly different yet; the pet on Home stops all its motion while
    another tab or screen is shown or the app is in the background.
  - 2026-10-09: the pet on Home moves part by part. A dog sways its tail and wags it in bursts
    (often when happy, now and then when content, and when you tap it), a cat swishes it slowly
    or holds it up with the tip quivering; both hang it when sad.
    The ears twitch now and then, droop when sad, perk up at a tap, and flap in a hop. The head
    tilts to listen while you hold the mic, nods while the pet speaks, and turns a little towards
    where the pet looks.
  - 2026-10-09: the pet's mouth moves when it talks: it opens with the loudness of its voice,
    with a little tongue when it opens wide, and shuts when the voice stops. A meow or a bark
    opens it once. Every mood has its own mouth now (a cat's little "w" when calm, a frown when
    sad), on the website's pets too.
  - 2026-10-09: where you touch the pet, it answers its own way. A pat on the head: it leans into
    your hand, ears back, eyes shut happily. A chin scratch: its head tips up and it purrs. A
    boop on the nose: it blinks and sneezes. An ear flicks; a tickled belly wriggles; a paw gives
    you a high five; touch its tail and it flicks it away and looks back at it. Hold a finger
    still on it for a hug. Each spot has its own lines, and VoiceOver has Pat head, Scratch chin,
    Rub belly and Hug.
  - 2026-10-09: the pet you meet at the end of Create is alive too. On "{name} is home" it
    breathes, blinks, moves its tail and ears, its mouth moves while it says its first line, and
    it answers a tap, a stroke and a hug as on Home. Its bubble keeps the first line. One new
    event, `pet_meet_touched`, says whether people touch the pet before taking it home.
  - 2026-10-09: the three pets on the first screen are alive as well. Each breathes, blinks,
    moves its tail and ears and answers a tap, a stroke and a hug. The ginger cat's mouth moves
    as each of its lines appears and the two beside it turn to look at it (no sound). A touch
    reaches the pet under your finger even where the three overlap. One new event,
    `welcome_pet_touched`, says whether people touch a pet before "Meet your pet".
  - 2026-10-12: the pet's look is softer (checked by Mert on his iPhone). Its eyes are bigger, with two
    glints. Its cheeks blush: more when it is happy, hardly when it is sad, and deeper for a
    moment when you pat its head, scratch its chin, stroke it or hug it. Cats have whiskers; cats
    and dogs with upright ears have a little fluff at the cheeks. The head and the body are lit
    from the upper left, in each fur's own colours, so they look round rather than flat. The
    website's pets and every small pet in the app (the builder, friends, portraits) have it too.
  - 2026-10-12: nothing new to see yet. Skia, the drawing engine the 2.5D pet needs, and the
    phone's tilt sensor are in the app's build, on the Simulator; the iPhone's build follows the
    evaluation. A phone with an older build keeps drawing today's pet. A screen for developers
    only compares two ways of lighting the pet, to choose one by how fast each draws on the
    iPhone.
  - 2026-10-12: the first try at the 2.5D pet's fur looked like wood grain (Mert). The
    developers' screen now shows the pet in six soft finishes to pick from: plush, squishy, felt,
    teddy, velvet and storybook.
  - 2026-10-12: of the six finishes Mert kept two, felt and storybook. Three more were built on
    them that look closer to 3D: each part of the pet casts a soft shadow on the next, the muzzle
    and the belly stand out, and the tail is round like a tube.
  - 2026-10-12: Mert picked Puppet. The pet drawn that way is alive now: it breathes, blinks,
    moves its tail, ears and head and answers a touch exactly as today's pet does, because both
    read the same pose. It is behind a developers' switch on Home (today's pet, the new one
    unlit, the new one in Puppet) until it has been seen on the iPhone.
