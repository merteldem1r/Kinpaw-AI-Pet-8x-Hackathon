For the lit, 2.5D pet (v3 pet looks, Part B), Mert wants **the feeling of a plush toy: soft and
sweet**. Fur drawn as fine streaks running down the coat he called "some kind of woody layer on
top of the pet" and rejected, in all three versions that had it.

**Why:** 2026-10-12, on the first lighting spike ([features/v3-pet-looks-plan.md](../application/features/v3-pet-looks-plan.md),
B0). A grain with a direction reads as wood on a flat, rounded shape; the product's feeling is
cozy ([[brand-dusk-lamp-amber]]).

**His picks so far (2026-10-12):** of six finishes he kept **Felt** (matte, a fine speckle) and
**Storybook** (warm light, cool shade), and dropped Plush, Squishy, Teddy and Velvet. Then he
asked for versions "most close to 3D with the separations of body, transitions on shadow":
parts that read as separate solids, with soft shadows where they meet.

**His pick (2026-10-12): Puppet**, the most 3D of the second round ("the closest for the living
pet 3D"): matte with a fine speckle, each part a soft solid that shadows the next. Its numbers
are `PUPPET` in `apps/mobile/lib/petLight.ts`.

**How to apply:** texture on the pet has no direction (speckle, soft tufts), shading is broad
and low in contrast, edges are soft, colours lean a little to pastel. When a look is a matter
of taste, show him several named versions side by side to pick from (a sheet, and a screen he
can tap through), rather than one to approve: he asked for "more interesting versions so I'm
gonna pick". How such a sheet is made without a device: [[skia-checks]].
