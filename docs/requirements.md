# Kinpaw: requirements

This play's requirements live in [`docs/application/`](application/), not here:

- [`product.md`](application/product.md): what Kinpaw is, who it is for, v1 scope, out of scope,
  key decisions and open questions.
- [`roadmap.md`](application/roadmap.md): the v1 milestones, then v2 and v3.
- [`UI/kinpaw-screen-spec-v1.md`](application/UI/kinpaw-screen-spec-v1.md): the screen-by-screen
  contract, including every event.
- [`features/`](application/features/): per-milestone requirements and plans from the `spec`
  skill.

The template's requirements scaffold that stood here is replaced by those files, so there is
one source of truth.
